## Scoring model

- **Quality pillar (60 pts):** ROE, ROIC, margins, leverage, interest coverage, liquidity, free cash flow, earnings quality.
- **Valuation pillar (40 pts):** margin of safety vs. a two-stage discounted owner-earnings intrinsic value, plus a P/E penalty for overpaying.
- **Verdict:** Strong Candidate >= 70 (and core gates pass), Watch >= 50, else Pass.
