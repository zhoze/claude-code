# Warren Buffett Screen - Ranked Candidates

> Educational screen, **not investment advice**. Buffett's real edge is qualitative (durable moat, honest management, circle of competence) and cannot be fully captured by ratios.

## Scoring model

- **Quality pillar (60 pts):** ROE, ROIC, margins, leverage, interest coverage, liquidity, free cash flow, earnings quality.
- **Valuation pillar (40 pts):** margin of safety vs. a two-stage discounted owner-earnings intrinsic value, plus a P/E penalty for overpaying.
- **Verdict:** Strong Candidate >= 70 (and core gates pass), Watch >= 50, else Pass.

## Forward-return estimates (model, not predictions)

- **Upside to value** = intrinsic ÷ price − 1 (total % to reach DCF fair value).
- **Exp. return/yr** = annualized return *if* price converges to intrinsic value over 5 years (intrinsic itself compounds at the discount rate).
- **Yrs to +35%** = time to a +35% gain at that expected annual return.
> ⚠️ These assume the price reaches the screen's *estimated* intrinsic value — a cheap stock can stay cheap, or the estimate can be wrong. Not a forecast of price or timing, and not investment advice.
