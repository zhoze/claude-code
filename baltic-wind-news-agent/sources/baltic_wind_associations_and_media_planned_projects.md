# Baltic Wind-Farm Development News Sources

**Geographic scope:** Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania  
**Content scope:** wind-energy associations and news organizations that have reported on planned, permitted, contracted or under-construction wind farms  
**Links checked:** 26 July 2026

> Some articles describe projects that have since advanced, changed ownership, been delayed or been completed. The links are retained because they document the development pipeline and the organizations that report on it.

## Recommended monitoring priority

For frequent monitoring of planned Baltic wind-farm projects:

1. **BalticWind.EU** — specialist Baltic project coverage.
2. **ERR, LSM and LRT** — national policy, permits, tenders and public debate.
3. **Äripäev, Dienas Bizness and 15min** — business, investment and construction reporting.
4. **OffshoreWIND.biz** — offshore tenders, surveys, design and permitting.
5. **EWPA, LWEA, LVEA and WindEurope** — association and policy updates.
6. **BNS and LETA** — original company and government announcements.
7. **Renewables Now, Windpower Monthly and Energy Global** — international investment, turbine-order and construction news.
