## 4. Company-name and ownership notes

- **GE Energy → GE Renewable Energy → GE Vernova:** the current group name is GE Vernova.
- **Siemens Wind Power → Siemens Gamesa:** historical Baltic turbines may be recorded under Siemens Wind Power.
- **Tacke Windtechnik:** acquired by Enron Wind; Enron Wind's wind business was subsequently acquired by GE.
- **Winwind and DeWind:** historical OEMs without current Baltic project pipelines.
- **Sumitomo Corporation:** no longer an Enefit Green partner in the Liivi Bay project as of the linked August 2025 announcement.
- **Ocean Winds:** exited Curonian Nord, with Ignitis Renewables taking full control.
- **European Energy → Energix:** the Jonava hybrid project was transferred to Energix in March 2026.
- **Eolus → Latvenergo:** Pienava was sold to Latvenergo, while Eolus continued project/construction management under the announced arrangement.
