# Baltic Municipalities Reporting on Wind-Farm Developments

**Geographic scope:** Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania  
**Source rule:** official city, municipality or county-municipality websites only  
**Links checked:** 26 July 2026

> This is a verified working list rather than a claim of absolute completeness. Municipal websites frequently reorganize their archives. The linked pages include municipal news articles, public-consultation notices, environmental-assessment notices and dedicated planning/project pages concerning proposed, permitted, under-construction or recently developed wind farms.
